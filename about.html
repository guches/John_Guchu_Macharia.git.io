How I approach transformation

Start with the journey

Map the real experience first (Customer Journey) with the AS-IS-Process mapping, including hand-offs, delays, duplicated effort and unclear ownership.

Make the operating model visible

Translate assumptions into workflows and Map the To-Be-Process, Map clear roles and responsibilities, Map Service Levels (Both Internal and External Stakeholders), Map risks and measurable outcomes.

Use technology as leverage

This leads to Systems Improvements , Digital Transformation, Process Automation and use of AI as methods and tools to remove friction rather than add another layer.

Close the feedback loop

Measure the outcommes and outputs through Operational Metrics, User Feedback, Lesson Learned and Stakeholder reviews to continuously shape the next Iteration and(or) Phase.
