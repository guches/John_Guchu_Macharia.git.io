KNBS × ONS Partnership & Digital Transformation

International programme leadership connecting statistical capability, digital public services and AI adoption.

Role
Programme Partnership Manager
Period
2022 – Jul 2026
Sector
Public sector / International development

Programme · AI · Data · Digital
Overview

This role connected the UK Office for National Statistics (ONS) with the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics and Somalia National Bureau of Statistics (SNBS) and their partners in Kenya and Somalia. The work combined partnership management, Stakeholder Management, Government Agencies Co-ordination, PMO disciplines, Stakeholder Co-ordination, Digital Transformation, Technical Capacity Building, Risk Managemnet and Budget oversight.

The context

The National Statistical institutions operate inside complex stakeholder environments i.e. Government Controlled or as a Standalone entity within their respective countries. They are also guided by their unique Technical priorities, Risk Scores, International partners, UK Funding Rules, Public Access to Data and Institutional Capability need that all needed to move together. The programme therefore required more than project tracking: it needed alignment between KNBS and SNBS Statistical Strategies, Delivery Models, Partnerships Agreements and User-facing digital services.

The agreement was based on Capacity Improvements both Technical and Digital Improvemnets. I managed the partnerships following 2 models i.e. In Kenya I was hosted by the organisation and for Somalia I worked remotely. Overally managed by the Strategic Adviser based in Rwanda and a Project Manager in the UK. For the Kenya Partnership we delivered Management Training to senior management and departmental managers. We also delivered Women in Leadership training where we delieverd management training to upcoming women in the path of leadership roles in both institutions.

In the Kenya (KNBS) Partnership I manged the improvement of their website. The website had vulnerabilities like Cross Site Scripting (XSS), Denial-of-Service (DoS / DDoS) and Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF) attacks. I started the process by running a Threat Intellligence analysis to get the full Threat Surface together with the KNBS IT team and ONS IT team. The report enabled us to get a clear way foward on what improvements needed to be done to the website. The decison was to re-do the website using the same infrasture i.e. word press so that we stay within the budget. I developed a business case for the project and also the project charter for approval.

After the set approval I floated a RFQ - Request for Quotaion from suppliers following the UK procurement processes. We managed to get a suppplier and their budget was also approved by the project sponsor i.e. ONS Senior Management. We got web developers that were within the set budget, this allowed us to onboard them on to our ERP system.

During the procurement process we also developed a Business Requiremets Document that captured what each department wasnted. The same was extended to customers through a focus group all in conjuction with the core project team (KNBS, KNBS Key Partners and Developers). We had to define what was inscope and what was out-of scope to manage stakeholders requirements.

I managed the project using a hybrid of Agile and Waterfall methodologies. We managed the project with monthly sprints after defining the backlog items with the developers, for the customer (KNBS) we had weekly meeting after delivery of a backlog item for them to test and give feedback. We also managed status using a shared folder to allow for teams to access the documents and give feedback. The delivery of the sprints allowed me to prepare their payments after delivery of the sprint and confirmation of done.

We delivered an upgraded website in 1 year after some issues arose some we had mapped and others came up later.

What the work involved

  • Managed the strategic partnership between KNBS, SNBS and ONS, aligning cross-organisational goals around national statistical standards and methodologies.
  • Coordinated international stakeholders including the World Bank, Stats Sweden, GPSDD and UN agencies across Kenya and Somalia initiatives.
  • Drove data and AI capacity-building programmes across Kenyan Ministries, Departments and Agencies.
  • Directed digital-transformation work including the KNBS website and Stats-Chat AI-driven bot.
  • Supported the national AI Challenge and emerging-technology integration within a government context.
  • Applied PMO practices around planning, risk, change control, benefits realisation, concept notes and business cases.
  • Monitored programme expenditure of approximately £100K per year with attention to ODA compliance.
  • Supported delivery of the 2026 Kenya Data Festival through milestones, stakeholder engagement and logistics.
